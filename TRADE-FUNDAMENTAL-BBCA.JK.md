## Company Overview

**PT Bank Central Asia Tbk (BBCA.JK)** is Indonesia's largest private bank by market capitalization and one of the most profitable financial institutions in Southeast Asia. Founded in 1957 and headquartered in Jakarta, BCA serves over 30 million customers through:

- **Retail banking:** Savings, current accounts, time deposits; leading brand for middle-class Indonesia
- **Commercial & corporate banking:** Working capital, investment loans, trade finance
- **Digital banking:** myBCA app, KlikBCA (internet banking), Halo BCA (contact center); market-leading digital adoption
- **Subsidiaries:** BCA Finance (auto financing), BCA Syariah (Islamic banking), BCA Sekuritas (securities), BCA Insurance

**Ownership:** Controlled by the Hartono brothers (Robert and Michael Hartono) through Djarum Group (~54.9%). The Hartonos are Indonesia's wealthiest individuals. Family-controlled governance with a long-term capital allocation track record.
