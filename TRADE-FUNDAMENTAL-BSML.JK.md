## Company Overview

**PT Bintang Samudera Mandiri Lines Tbk (BSML.JK)** is a small Indonesian sea transportation and logistics company founded in 2007 and headquartered in Jakarta Selatan. The company, together with its subsidiary PT Bintang Samudera Mandiri Persada, provides:

- **Sea freight transport:** Coal, nickel ore, cement, sand, pipes, construction equipment, fertilizers, and clinkers
- **Logistics services:** Transshipment, barge loading, ship cargo, ramp door/trestle rental
- **Marine services:** Shipping agency, bunkering, loading/unloading, refueling, ship management

**Fleet:** 8 tugboat units + 7 barge units

**Operating regions:** Sumatra, Kalimantan, Java, Sulawesi
