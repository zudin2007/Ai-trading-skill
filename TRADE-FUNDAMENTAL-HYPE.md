## Protocol Overview

Hyperliquid is a Layer-1 blockchain optimized for high-performance decentralized perpetuals trading. It operates as a fully on-chain order book with CEX-level performance (~50,000 orders/second, sub-millisecond latency), enabling it to compete directly with centralized exchanges like Binance and Bybit on user experience while maintaining full self-custody.

**Core value proposition:** The first DEX to meaningfully challenge centralized perpetuals exchanges at scale — not by replicating AMM mechanics, but by building a purpose-built execution layer.
