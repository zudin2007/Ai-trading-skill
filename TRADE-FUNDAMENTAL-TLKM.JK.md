## Business Overview

PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk is Indonesia's state-owned telecommunications holding company, the country's largest by revenue, market cap, and subscriber base. Founded 1884 (predecessor), listed on IDX 1995 and NYSE (ADR: TLK) simultaneously. Government (Republic of Indonesia) holds ~52.1% controlling stake.

### Business Segments

| Segment | Key Asset | Position | Revenue Contribution |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| Mobile | Telkomsel | #1 mobile operator, ~60% market share | ~50% of revenue |
| Fixed Broadband | IndiHome (now in Telkomsel) | #1 fixed broadband; 10.8M subscribers | ~15% |
| Enterprise | Telkom Digital, cloud, IT | Growing B2B | ~20% |
| Infrastructure | Fiber network (→ TIF) | 583,000 km total fiber | ~15% |

### Fixed-Mobile Convergence (FMC)
The defining strategic move of 2024–2025: IndiHome (fixed broadband with 10.8M subscribers) was merged into Telkomsel (mobile) to create a converged fixed+mobile product. Penetration of convergence services reached **56%** by end-2024. This mirrors the successful FMC strategies of Singtel, Telstra, and European carriers that re-rated their stocks on convergence.

## Key Risks

### 1. SEC/DOJ FCPA Investigation — Material Risk
- Telkom is subject to ongoing SEC and DOJ probes into Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) compliance and accounting controls
- FCPA penalties for state-controlled telcos in emerging markets have historically resulted in fines of $100M–$1B+ (e.g., VimpelCom $795M, Ericsson $1B)
- **Risk:** Large cash fine reduces dividend capacity; reputation damage; possible NYSE delisting of ADR (TLK)
- **Mitigant:** State-owned enterprise status reduces risk of criminal charges vs. private firms; Indonesian government incentivized to resolve

### 2. Tax Assessment — IDR 14.57 Trillion
- October 2025: IDR 14,566 billion (~$910M) tax underpayment assessment related to IndiHome business transfer
- If upheld in full, this represents ~70% of annual net profit — devastating for dividend capacity
- **Risk:** Tax underpayment payments could span 1–3 years of impaired dividends
- **Mitigant:** TLKM is appealing; Indonesian tax authority assessments are frequently reduced on appeal; government would not destroy its own state-owned champion's dividend capacity

### 3. Revenue Decline Trajectory
- 9M25 revenue down -2.3% y/y; operating profit -10.6%; net profit -10.7%
- If revenue continues declining in 2026F at similar rate, EBITDA margins could compress further
- **Risk:** Multiple compression if market prices in permanently lower earnings power
- **Mitigant:** FMC, TIF spinoff, enterprise growth are structural offsets

### 4. IDR/USD Weakness
- IDR at ~17,400/USD (near historical lows)
- USD-denominated costs (equipment, spectrum, satellite) increase in IDR terms
- For foreign investors, USD return = IDR return minus IDR depreciation
- **Risk:** If IDR depreciates further toward 18,000–19,000/USD, foreign investors face FX headwinds
- **Mitigant:** Telkom earns 100% in IDR; FX risk is primarily to foreign investors, not the business

### 5. Macro: IDX Weakness + MSCI Outflows
- JKSE composite down -12% over 4 weeks; MSCI EM rebalancing forces ~$1.8B outflows from IDX
- TLKM is a top-5 IDX constituent; institutional forced selling hits large-caps disproportionately
- **Risk:** Outflows continue through end of June 2026, keeping TLKM under pressure
- **Mitigant:** This is temporary; once rebalancing completes, fundamental buyers return for the yield

5. **State-backed stability** — 52.1% government ownership = implicit guarantee against bankruptcy; dividend continuity incentivized by government's fiscal needs.

2. **FCPA probe + IDR 14.57T tax risk** — two large contingent liabilities that could consume 1–3 years of annual profit if resolved adversely.
3. **ROE declining** — from historical avg 19% to current 16.6%; gradual erosion of capital efficiency.
4. **FBB price war** — analysts flagging competitive pressure in fixed broadband that could further compress margins.
5. **Low growth ceiling** — as Indonesia's dominant telco, growth is inherently limited by market saturation; not a growth stock.
