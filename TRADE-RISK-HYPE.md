## Protocol / Smart Contract Risk

### Technical Risk Assessment

**Hyperliquid runs on a custom Layer-1 blockchain** — not on an established L1 like Ethereum or Solana. This introduces:

| Risk Factor | Assessment | Severity |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| Smart contract bugs | Custom consensus + order book = novel attack surface | High |
| Oracle manipulation | On-chain price oracles for liquidations; subject to flash loan attacks | Moderate |
| Liquidity cascade | High-leverage perps platform; liquidations can amplify moves | High |
| Validator concentration | Hyperliquid validator set details not fully public | Moderate |
| Bridge risk | Cross-chain deposits/withdrawals use bridges (bridge hacks are common) | Moderate |

**Historical record (positive):** No major exploit has been reported in Hyperliquid's 19-month operational history. The system has processed $1.6 trillion in cumulative volume without a critical failure. However, 19 months is insufficient history to conclude the protocol is fully battle-hardened at this scale.

**Liquidation cascade risk:** A perps platform with $7.3B in open interest is inherently exposed to cascading liquidations during rapid market moves. If BTC drops 20% rapidly, leveraged HYPE/BTC longs could be liquidated en masse, pushing HYPE down far more than BTC.
