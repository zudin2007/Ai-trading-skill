## Legal & Regulatory Risk — The Key Risks

### Risk 1: SEC/DOJ FCPA Investigation

**What it is:**
- US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and Department of Justice (DOJ) are investigating Telkom Indonesia for potential violations of the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)
- FCPA prohibits US-listed companies (Telkom has ADR: TLK on NYSE) from bribing foreign officials
- Investigation also covers accounting controls integrity

**Precedent fines for EM telcos under FCPA:**

| Company | Year | Fine |
|---------|------|------|
| VimpelCom (Russia) | 2016 | $795M |
| Magyar Telekom (Hungary) | 2011 | $95M |
| Ericsson (telecoms equipment) | 2019 | $1.06B |
| MTS (Russia) | 2019 | $850M |

**Potential TLKM exposure:** Given Telkom's size ($18B market cap) and precedents, a fine in the range of **$100M–$800M** is the reference range.

**Impact at various fine levels:**
- $100M (~IDR 1.6T): Manageable; ~7.6% of annual net profit
- $300M (~IDR 4.8T): Significant; ~22.9% of annual net profit; dividend sustainability questioned
- $800M (~IDR 12.8T): Severe; ~61.1% of annual net profit; likely forces dividend cut or suspension

**Timeline:** FCPA investigations typically take 2–5 years to resolve from initiation. No charges have been filed as of June 2026.

**Mitigation:** State-owned enterprise status (government is 52.1% owner) creates strong political incentive for a negotiated settlement rather than prosecution; Indonesian government would be deeply reluctant to allow prosecution of its national champion.

### Risk 2: IDR 14,566 Billion Tax Assessment

**What it is:**
- October 2025: Indonesian tax authority (DJP) issued assessment for IDR 14,566 billion (~$910M) underpayment
- Related to the IndiHome business transfer from Telkom to Telkomsel
- Telkom is formally appealing; appeal timeline: 1–3 years

**Impact if upheld in full:**
- IDR 14,566B = ~70% of FY25 estimated annual net profit (~IDR 21T)
- Would require either: (a) dividend suspension for 1–2 years, or (b) taking on additional debt
- Note: Indonesian tax authorities often settle for 40–60% of assessed amount on appeal

**Impact if partially reduced (40–60% resolution):**
- IDR 5.8–8.7T payment → ~28–42% of annual net profit
- Dividend likely maintained but reduced; stock re-rates lower but not catastrophically

**Key observation:** The stock has already fallen -25.8% from its peak partly in anticipation of this risk. The market may have over-discounted the worst case.

## State-Owned Enterprise (SOE) Backstop

**The single most important risk mitigant for TLKM:**
- Government of Indonesia owns 52.1% → can never be hostile to TLKM
- Government derives meaningful fiscal revenue from TLKM dividends (~IDR 11T/year based on stake × dividend)
- Political incentive: allowing TLKM to fail or slash dividends would embarrass the government
- Implicit guarantee: TLKM can access Bank Indonesia / state bank credit lines on favorable terms
- No bankruptcy risk

This SOE backstop is a structural risk limiter that separates TLKM from private-sector telcos. **The probability of a catastrophic outcome (total loss, delisting) is very low.**
