### What the CLARITY Act Does

The CLARITY Act resolves the decade-long jurisdictional war between the SEC and CFTC over digital assets by creating a three-category framework:
- **Digital Commodities** (Bitcoin, Ethereum) → CFTC oversight
- **Investment Contract Assets** → SEC oversight
- **Payment Stablecoins** → Banking regulators

For Bitcoin specifically, CFTC classification is the preferred outcome — the CFTC has historically been more markets-friendly. This regulatory clarity would remove a major overhang on institutional adoption, potentially accelerating ETF expansion, corporate treasury allocations, and bank custody services.

**Assessment:** With a 59% Polymarket probability and a hard July 4 deadline, the CLARITY Act is the single biggest near-term catalyst for Bitcoin. Every week without passage increases risk of the August recess killing the bill for 2026. The market is not yet pricing in a full "passage" premium — successful passage could add 10–20% to BTC price.
