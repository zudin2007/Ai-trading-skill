## The Core Thesis

### The Value/Income Case

**Why TLKM is interesting at IDR 2,960:**

1. **7.2% dividend yield** — IDR 212.47/share just went ex-dividend June 11; the next cycle is already accruing. At 5.6% annual DPS growth, yield-on-cost rises to ~8.9% within 5 years. For an Indonesian domestic investor in a 4–5% deposit-rate environment, this is compelling income.

2. **EV/EBITDA 4.1x** — the market is pricing TLKM as if it is a declining business in terminal decay. For an operator with 60% mobile market share, national infrastructure concessions, and a state-backed balance sheet, 4.1x EV/EBITDA historically represents deep value. The implied mean-reversion to 5.5x EV/EBITDA alone would deliver ~35% stock appreciation.

3. **61.8% Fibonacci support** — the current IDR 2,960 price is testing the golden ratio retracement of the 52-week swing (IDR 2,977). Historically, this level acts as a high-probability bounce zone for fundamentally sound large-cap stocks in correction.

4. **TIF fiber spinoff (H2 2026)** — transferring IDR 35.8T of infrastructure assets to a separate entity crystallizes hidden value. Infrastructure assets trade at higher EV/EBITDA than integrated operators; the spinoff is a re-rating catalyst.

5. **MSCI forced selling is fading** — the ~$1.8B mechanical outflow from IDX is mostly complete; the structural income buyer should return for TLKM's yield at current levels.
